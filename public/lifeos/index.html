Systems

Deep Simulation, Not Scripted

Inherited Appearance

Skin tone, hair colour and eye colour are drawn from a phenotype model and passed down the family tree, so children resemble the parents who made them.

Event-Sourced LifeLog

Every stat change, age-up, purchase and decision is logged as an immutable event. Stats are projections computed from the trail — no fragile save states.

Yarn Narrative Engine

21 Yarn scripts and 104 story nodes power branching storylines, with narrative layers assembled from current state, health, relationships and recent events.

Dynamic Audio

Five background tracks transition with game state, alongside UI selection sounds and a game-over sting. Powered by Audioplayers with background playback.

Animation-Rich UI

Rive vector animations on the nav and stat bars, Flutter Animate transitions, shimmer loading states and confetti celebrations. Every interaction has weight.

Supernatural & Political Overlays

Optional depth layers over any life: vampire covens with bloodline tracking, cult devotion mechanics, royal succession and political influence networks.

Built With

Architecture

Flutter + Dart

One codebase on Flutter 3.44. Android is the release target; other platform projects exist but aren't in the release gate.

Riverpod

Four provider patterns — singleton services, async data, real-time streams, and parameterized family providers.

Drift + SQLite

47 tables at schema v79, with idempotent migrations. Query-heavy by design — no wasteful writes.

Rive + Flutter Animate

Vector animation runtime paired with a declarative animation library. No video assets anywhere.
