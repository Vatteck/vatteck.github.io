Privacy Policy

This policy explains how LifeOS, the life simulation game published by VATTECK on Google Play, handles your data. It applies only to the LifeOS Android app, and not to any other VATTECK application.

LifeOS - Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Summary

LifeOS collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, and no network access. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

2. What the App Itself Stores

Your characters, life events, relationships, careers, assets, and game settings are stored on your device in a local database and a local preferences file.

This data is never transmitted anywhere. There is no cloud save, no account system, and no way for VATTECK to see it.

3. No Network Access

LifeOS requests no runtime permissions. The INTERNET permission has been removed from the app manifest, so the app is not capable of making network requests at all.

4. No Ads, Purchases, or Analytics

LifeOS contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not use Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, or any third-party SDK that collects data.

Because no third-party SDK receives anything, there is no advertising identifier collected, and no consent flow is required in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland.

5. What the App Does Not Access

LifeOS does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, calendar, call logs, or device identifiers.

6. Data Sharing

None. The app does not sell, share, or transmit any data, because it does not collect or transmit any data.

7. Your Controls

Uninstalling LifeOS removes all of its saved data from your device. You can also clear its data from Android settings without uninstalling.

There is no server-side copy of anything, so there is no deletion request to make and nothing for VATTECK to delete on your behalf.

8. Children's Privacy

LifeOS is rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Google Play

Google Play collects its own data when you download or update an app, independently of LifeOS. That is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by this one.

10. Fiction Disclaimer

LifeOS is a work of satire and fiction. Names, characters, businesses, and events in the game are fictitious, and any resemblance to real persons, living or dead, is coincidental. References to real-world brands are used for parody and do not imply affiliation with or endorsement by those brands.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted at this URL.
